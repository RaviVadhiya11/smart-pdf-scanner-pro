Request DocNexa account deletion

You can request deletion of your DocNexa account and the PDFs synced to DocNexa Cloud without using the Android app.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email from the email address registered with DocNexa.
  2. Use the subject DocNexa account deletion request.
  3. Include the DocNexa account email address. Never include your password.
  4. We may reply to verify that you own the account before processing the request.

Email deletion request

What is deleted

  • Your DocNexa Supabase authentication account, after ownership is verified and the deletion is completed.
  • PDFs synced to the DocNexa Cloud storage folder belonging to that account.

What is not deleted

  • PDFs stored locally inside the DocNexa app on your phone.
  • Copies you exported or shared to Downloads, Drive, email or another app.
  • Information that a service provider must retain for legal, security or fraud-prevention reasons, where applicable.

To remove local PDFs, delete them inside DocNexa or clear/uninstall the app. Save any documents you want to keep before requesting account deletion.

For help or questions, contact business@pixelfirestudio.in. Do not send your password or authentication codes.
