DocNexa Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

This policy explains how DocNexa – PDF Scanner & Editor handles information when you use the Android app.

1. Information and account details

If you create or use a DocNexa account, Supabase processes your email address, account identifier, authentication credentials and session tokens to provide sign-in, email confirmation, password recovery, cloud storage and account deletion. Authentication passwords are handled by the authentication provider; do not share passwords or verification codes with support.

2. Documents, scans and images

Scans and documents are processed to provide scanning, PDF creation, editing, OCR, conversion and related features. Documents saved inside the app are stored in private app storage on your device. When you choose to export or share a file, Android passes the selected file to the destination you choose. Local document operations are intended to remain on-device unless you choose a feature requiring a remote service, such as Cloud Sync or AI processing.

DocNexa requires an active, validated internet connection to use the app. Local processing remains on-device unless you explicitly select a remote feature. The optional App Lock uses Android device credentials; DocNexa does not collect or store your device PIN, pattern or password. When App Lock is enabled, screenshots are blocked in DocNexa-owned windows. App Lock is not document-file encryption.

3. Optional cloud sync

If you sign in and explicitly use Cloud Sync, DocNexa uploads the PDFs selected for sync to a private Supabase Storage bucket, in a folder associated with your account ID. Cloud files are used to restore your synced PDFs. Signing out does not delete cloud copies. You can request account deletion in the app; this removes the DocNexa cloud account and its synced cloud PDFs when the deletion service completes successfully. Local files on your phone are not deleted by cloud account deletion.

Google Drive is optional. Manual file-picker exports are handled by your selected Android storage provider. If you authorize direct Google Drive access, Google processes the selected account and grants access to files used with DocNexa through the drive.file scope. DocNexa sends only files you explicitly choose, uses short-lived access tokens without persisting them, and can retrieve app-created backups associated with the current DocNexa account. Google Drive copies remain in your Google account until you delete them there; removing authorization or deleting your DocNexa account does not delete those copies. You can manage Google authorization in your Google Account.

4. AI document features

If you choose an AI feature for a document, the extracted document text and the prompt or task needed for that feature are sent over HTTPS to DocNexa's Supabase-hosted AI service for processing. Do not submit confidential or sensitive information unless you are comfortable with that processing. AI output may be inaccurate and should be checked.

5. Camera, photos and file access

Camera access is used when you choose live scanning or QR scanning. The system document scanner and selected image or PDF files are used only to perform the feature you request. File selection uses Android's document picker; the app does not need broad access to all files on your device.

6. Analytics, diagnostics, configuration and notifications

DocNexa integrates Firebase Analytics for app usage events, Crashlytics for crash diagnostics, Performance Monitoring for app performance, Remote Config for app announcements/settings, and Firebase Cloud Messaging for optional service announcements. These services may process app instance identifiers, device/app information, diagnostic data and notification tokens as required to operate their features. You can control notifications through Android settings.

7. Advertising and consent choices

DocNexa displays Google Mobile Ads, including native ads. Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, app interaction and advertising information to deliver, measure and protect ads, subject to your device settings and Google's policies. DocNexa uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present privacy consent messages where required and provides a Privacy choices entry in the app menu when available. You can review or change your consent choices using that entry. The app contains ads.

7a. Optional Google Play subscriptions

When subscriptions are available and you choose to purchase one, Google Play handles payment and renewal. DocNexa sends the Google Play purchase token and authenticated DocNexa account information to its verification service to check subscription status, expiry and acknowledgement. We use a hashed account identifier to associate the purchase with your DocNexa account. DocNexa does not receive your full payment card details. You can manage or cancel renewal in Google Play subscriptions. Cancellation does not normally end access before the paid period expires. Internet access and account sign-in are required for membership verification.

8. Sharing and service providers

Information is processed by the service providers described above (including Supabase, Google Firebase, Google Play services/ML Kit and Google Mobile Ads) to provide app functionality, security, diagnostics and advertising. We do not sell your documents. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or to protect users and the service.

9. Retention and deletion

Local documents remain on your device until you delete them, clear app data or uninstall the app. Exported copies may remain in Downloads or other locations you choose. Cloud documents remain until you delete them through available account features or complete account deletion. Diagnostic and advertising data may be retained by the relevant service providers under their own retention policies.

To request deletion of your DocNexa account and associated cloud data, use the DocNexa account deletion page. Account deletion does not remove local files or copies previously exported to other apps or folders.

10. Your privacy choices

You can choose not to sign in or use optional cloud and AI features. You can manage Android camera and notification permissions in device settings. Where available, use the in-app Privacy choices control to revisit advertising consent. To request account and associated cloud data deletion, see the account deletion instructions. Requests about personal information may be sent to the contact below.

11. Children

DocNexa is not designed for children and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Security

Network requests to DocNexa services use HTTPS. App-private files are stored in Android app storage. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure; protect your device and account credentials.

13. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app or its service providers change. For privacy questions, contact business@pixelfirestudio.in.

Please review this policy and the app's actual Firebase, Supabase and AdMob configuration before publishing. The Play Console Data safety answers must match the SDKs and features enabled in the released build.
